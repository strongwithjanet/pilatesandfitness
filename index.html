Meet your instructor

Hi, I'm Janet.

I'm a mom of three, a NASM Certified Personal Trainer, and a Certified Balanced Body Mat Pilates Instructor. My fitness journey began when I decided to change my habits, nourish my body, and move consistently.

After going from 200 pounds to 140 pounds, I discovered that strength is about so much more than a number. I'm deeply grateful to God for the transformation in my life, and I hope to inspire others to believe in themselves, too.
